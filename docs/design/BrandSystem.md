# Brand System

## Brand Position

VC-WMS is a premium workforce management SaaS platform for companies that need trustworthy attendance, payroll, leave, and employee operations without operational clutter.

The brand should feel:

- Precise like Linear.
- Commercially polished like Stripe.
- Calm and structured like Notion.
- Fast and technical like Vercel.
- Operationally complete like Rippling.
- Financially trustworthy like Ramp.
- Slightly organic through color, spacing, and language, without looking rustic.

## Brand Promise

Reliable workforce operations for every location, every shift, and every tenant.

## Design Personality

- Clear before clever.
- Calm before decorative.
- Fast before theatrical.
- Premium before flashy.
- Human before bureaucratic.

## Visual Direction

Use a modern SaaS foundation with restrained organic cues:

- Soft off-white and graphite surfaces.
- Fresh green as the primary product accent.
- Clean typography.
- Fine borders.
- Compact dashboards.
- Confident whitespace.
- Subtle motion.
- No generic purple AI gradients.
- No heavy glassmorphism.
- No playful HR cartoon style.

## White-Label Position

The base product must remain visually excellent even before tenant branding is applied.

Tenant branding can change:

- Logo.
- Tenant display name.
- Primary accent.
- Secondary accent.
- Login page brand panel.
- PWA app name.
- Favicon.

Tenant branding must not change:

- Core layout density.
- Accessibility thresholds.
- Attendance flow hierarchy.
- Error semantics.
- Component behavior.
- Security-critical visual states.

## Voice

Product language should be direct, calm, and operational.

Use:

- "Check in"
- "Approve leave"
- "Generate payroll"
- "Outside location"
- "Face verification failed"
- "Needs review"

Avoid:

- Cute HR phrases.
- Overly legalistic warnings.
- Decorative marketing language inside operational flows.
- Blaming users for device, GPS, or camera failures.

## Product Naming

Platform name:

- VC-WMS

Tenant display:

- Tenant name appears in the app shell, login screen, PWA install prompt, exports, and email templates.

Role names:

- Platform Super Admin.
- Tenant Owner.
- HR Admin.
- Manager.
- Employee.

## Brand Rules

- The first viewport of login must show the tenant identity.
- Internal screens prioritize task completion over brand display.
- Brand color is an accent, not a full-page wash.
- Tenant logos must sit in fixed containers to avoid layout shifts.
- Critical states use semantic colors, not tenant brand colors.
